Health and fitness calculators

The body mass index calculator divides weight in kilograms by height in metres squared. BMI is a screening ratio for populations, not a measure of body fat or fitness, and the page says so next to the result. The BMR calculator and TDEE calculator estimate resting and total daily energy use with the Mifflin–St Jeor equation, and the macro calculator splits a calorie target into protein, fat and carbohydrate. Every health estimator states its limits and when to ask a clinician.

Frequently asked questions

Is the calculator free to use?

Yes. Every calculator is free, with no sign-up and no paywall. Inputs are processed in your browser and are never uploaded.

How is EMI calculated?

The fixed monthly instalment is EMI = P × r × (1+r)n / ((1+r)n − 1), where P is the principal, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of months. See how EMI is calculated for a worked example.

How is body mass index calculated?

Body mass index is weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared. Read the BMI formula and its limitations before treating the number as a health verdict.

What is the difference between simple and compound interest?

Simple interest pays only on the original principal. Compound interest pays interest on accumulated interest, so the amount grows faster. See how compound interest works.
